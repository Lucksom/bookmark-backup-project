​​Bookmark Cloud Backup ☁️🔖

​✨ Why you will love it:
​☁️ Your Own Private Cloud
Instead of trusting a random third-party server, this extension saves your bookmarks securely in a dedicated folder right inside your own Google Drive.

​🧠 Smart Merge (No More Duplicates!)
We hate messy bookmarks as much as you do. When you back up or restore, the extension actually checks what you already have and only adds the new links. No annoying duplicate folders or doubled-up links to clean up later.

​⏱️ Set It and Forget It
Once you connect your account, you don't even have to think about it. The extension quietly runs in the background and saves a fresh backup of your latest bookmarks every 6 hours automatically.

​🎛️ You Are In Control
Want to safely add some new links to your existing backup? Choose "Update (Merge)". Want to completely wipe the old cloud backup and start fresh? Choose "Replace". You have total control over how your files are saved.

​💾 Works Offline Too
Going off the grid or moving to a new browser without Google access? You can easily export and import your bookmarks directly to your phone or computer storage as a simple file.

​📱 Beautiful on Mobile
We built this with phone users in mind! It features a sleek, frosted-glass design that feels like a native app, and even brings in your actual Google profile picture and email once you log in.
